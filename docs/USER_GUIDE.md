# Smart Inbox - User Guide

Welcome to Smart Inbox! This guide will help you get started and make the most of your AI-powered email assistant.

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up & Connect Gmail

1. Visit the Smart Inbox homepage and click "Sign in with Google"
2. Select your Google account (the one with your real estate emails)
3. Grant permissions:
   - **Read emails** - Required for AI processing
   - **Send emails** - Optional, enables 1-click replies

### 2. Train Your AI (2-5 minutes)

After connecting, you'll be guided through AI training:

1. **Tone Selection** - Choose your communication style:
   - Professional
   - Friendly
   - Casual
   - Direct

2. **Example Replies** - Provide 2-3 example replies you've sent
   - This teaches the AI your voice

3. **Rules & Preferences** - Set custom rules:
   - "Never auto-reply to legal emails"
   - "Always flag emails from [specific client]"
   - "Pre-approved = hot lead"

### 3. First Sync

Click **"Sync Gmail"** then **"Run AI"** to process your inbox. The first run may take a few minutes depending on email volume.

## Daily Workflow

### Morning: Check Your Digest

The **Daily Digest** page shows your morning brief:
- **Hot leads** - Pre-approved buyers, ready-to-list sellers
- **Closings & deadlines** - Time-sensitive transactions
- **Overdue follow-ups** - Clients waiting for your response
- **Other** - Everything else

### Throughout the Day: Smart Inbox

The **Inbox** page shows threads prioritized by AI:

**Priority Indicators:**
- 🔴 **90-100** - Urgent (hot lead, closing today)
- 🟠 **70-89** - High (follow-up needed, client question)
- 🟡 **50-69** - Medium (routine updates)
- 🟢 **<50** - Low (newsletters, spam)

**Quick Actions per Thread:**
1. Click thread to view full conversation
2. Review AI summary at top
3. Edit/send AI draft reply (1-click)
4. Accept/dismiss suggested action items

### Thread View

Click any thread to see:
- **Full email history** with AI summaries
- **Sentiment indicators** (positive/neutral/negative)
- **AI draft reply** - Edit and send
- **Action items** - Convert to calendar events
- **Pipeline hints** - Buyer/seller signals

### Action Items

The **Action Items** page consolidates all extracted tasks:
- Filter by type: Call, Calendar, Todo, Email, Follow-up
- Sort by due date
- Mark complete when done

### Alerts

The **Alerts** page shows real-time notifications:
- Hot lead detected
- Closing deadline approaching
- Client complaint/negative sentiment
- Urgent follow-up needed

Click "Mark read" or "Open thread" to act.

## Team Features (Team Plan)

### Create a Team

1. Go to **Teams** → "Create Team"
2. Enter team name (e.g., "Smith Realty Group")

### Invite Members

1. Select your team
2. Click "Invite Member"
3. Choose role:
   - **Admin** - Full access, manage team
   - **Agent** - Access shared inbox only

### Shared Inbox

Team members see:
- All team threads (with owner indicators)
- Shared action items
- Team digest (aggregated)
- Team reports

### Team Reports

View **Reports** for team performance:
- Emails processed per agent
- Actions created
- Category breakdown
- Priority distribution
- Response times

## Tips & Best Practices

### Improve AI Accuracy

1. **Mark drafts as "Sent" or "Rejected"** - Teaches AI your preferences
2. **Edit drafts before sending** - AI learns from your edits
3. **Use consistent tone** - Don't mix formal/casual randomly

### Email Management

1. **Archive processed threads** - Keeps inbox clean
2. **Use "Run AI" daily** - Processes new emails overnight
3. **Check digest each morning** - Never miss hot leads

### Team Collaboration

1. **Assign action items** - Tag team members in descriptions
2. **Use shared labels** - Consistent categorization
3. **Weekly team review** - Check reports together

## Troubleshooting

### "Monthly limit reached"
- Upgrade plan in Billing
- Or wait for monthly reset (1st of month)

### "Gmail connection failed"
- Reconnect in sidebar (+ Connect Gmail)
- Check Google Cloud console for API quotas

### "AI draft looks wrong"
- Edit and send - AI learns from corrections
- Add more example replies in Preferences

### Missing emails
- Click "Sync Gmail" to fetch latest
- Check spam folder in Gmail

### Team member can't see threads
- Verify they're added to team
- Check they have "Agent" or "Admin" role
- Ensure they've connected their own Gmail
